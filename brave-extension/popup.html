ClipBridge Auto Attach

Pair with ClipBridge Windows and automatically place prepared clips in your selected ChatGPT conversation.

Not paired

Sign in to ChatGPT normally in Brave first. Open the conversation you want, then arm that tab. Clips are offered together, up to your chosen count and 1 GB per batch. ChatGPT may enforce a lower limit. Auto-send is off by default. When enabled, it clicks Send once only after visible readiness checks, then waits for a new user message and response completion. Errors, edits, navigation or uncertain results pause the queue.
